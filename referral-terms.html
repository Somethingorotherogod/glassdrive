Referral Terms

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

  1. Every GlassDrive account has a personal referral code.
  2. Your friend chooses “A friend told me” in the app and enters your code. When they make their first subscription payment, you earn 1 free month.
  3. Rewards stack: there's no limit to how many free months you can earn, but we may review unusually high numbers of referrals. They work on Pro and Ultimate.
  4. Free months are added after your current paid period. Your billing is paused for that time and you keep full access. If you aren't subscribed yet, they're saved and applied once you are.
  5. Free months don't count towards loyalty price drops. Your loyalty progress picks up where it left off when you start paying again.
  6. Entering a referral code also counts as answering “How did you find out about us?”, so your friend gets the full 14-day trial.
  7. Rewards have no cash value and can't be transferred or exchanged.
  8. You can't refer yourself, create accounts to earn rewards, or use spam to share your code. If a referred payment is refunded or charged back, or a referral breaks these terms, we may remove the reward.
  9. We may change or end the programme with 30 days' notice. Free months you've already earned will still be honoured.

*After the beta ends. Free trial: 7 days when you create your account, plus 7 more when you answer one quick question in the app (“How did you find out about us?”). Everyone who answers gets the full 14 days, and no card is needed. The trial includes every Pro feature; Ultimate's custom backgrounds are for subscribers.
