Download GlassDrive Beta

GlassDrive-Beta.dmg · about 3 MB · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · free during the beta

About a minute, and only once

Installing GlassDrive.

1

Open the download

Find GlassDrive-Beta.dmg in your Downloads folder (or click it in your browser's downloads) and double-click it.

2

Drag it into Applications

A window opens with an arrow. Drag GlassDrive onto the Applications folder, then you can close the window.

3

Allow it, just once

Open GlassDrive from Applications. macOS says it can't check it: click Done, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

4

You're in

GlassDrive opens like any other app from now on, even after updates. Create your account and you're in the beta.

Want to be extra sure the file is genuine? In Terminal run shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/GlassDrive-Beta.dmg and check it matches: [CHECKSUM]

Why macOS will warn you

It won't show as a
“trusted” Apple app.

When you open GlassDrive the first time, macOS will say it can't verify the developer. That's because we don't pay for Apple's Developer Programme. It costs £79 a year, and all we'd get for it is a seal of approval.

To keep our prices as low as possible, we put that money into building the app instead. We'll join as soon as GlassDrive earns enough, and the warning will disappear.

*After the beta ends. Free trial: 7 days when you create your account, plus 7 more when you answer one quick question in the app (“How did you find out about us?”). Everyone who answers gets the full 14 days, and no card is needed. The trial includes every Pro feature; Ultimate's custom backgrounds are for subscribers.
