GlassDrive Beta

Features.

Everything you need to keep your Mac tidy, private and easy to use.

Browsing

Feels like Drive. Works like a Mac.

Folders on top, big previews below, Recent and Starred in the sidebar. Switch to list view and sort by any column. Search looks through every folder you've imported in an instant.

Finder sync

Your real files. Always in sync.

GlassDrive doesn't copy or upload anything. It works directly on your files, so every change shows up in Finder immediately. Stars are saved as Finder tags too.

Activity & Revert

Undo, for everything.

Renamed the wrong thing? Moved a folder by mistake? Every change is listed with a Revert button, and deleted items always go to the Trash first.

Confidential

Private folders, properly locked.

Create password-locked folders encrypted with AES-256, or lock an existing one. They lock automatically when your Mac sleeps or the screen locks. Wrong passwords are slowed down and logged, and GlassDrive never stores your password.

Storage Offloader

Space back, without the hunting.

Pick how long a file can go unopened, from a week to two years. Choose which kinds of files count. Protect anything with ⋯ › Never offload. Review the list, then offload to the Trash, and undo with one click.

App Controls

Every app, under control.

See what's installed, how big it is and what's running. Install from a .dmg or .app in one click. Uninstall moves the app and its leftover files to the Trash, and you can put them back.

Appearance

Light, dark and glass.

Follows your Mac's light or dark mode. The frost slider takes the glass from completely clear to fully frosted. With macOS Tahoe or later, GlassDrive uses Apple's own Liquid Glass.

Ultimate

Custom backgrounds.

Choose any photo and GlassDrive puts it behind the glass. Scale it up or down until it sits just right. It's the one thing Ultimate adds: a little more customisation, and an extra high five.

Permissions

Allow once. Done.

Instead of macOS asking for every folder and drive, GlassDrive walks you through one switch, Full Disk Access, so it never needs to ask again.
