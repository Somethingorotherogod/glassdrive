GlassDrive Beta

Privacy.
That's the whole point.

GlassDrive is built so we know as little about you as possible.

Your files stay on your Mac

No uploads, no cloud copies, no scanning. Your files, file names and folders never leave your Mac.

No tracking. At all.

No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs in the app, and no cookies or trackers on this website.

Only what your account needs

Your email, your subscription, and anything you choose to tell us (like how you found us or feedback). Anonymous crash reports can be switched off. Never your files or your Mac's name.

Passwords we can't see

Confidential folders use Apple's built-in AES-256 encryption. We never store your password and can't recover it.

Hidden from Spotlight

Locked folders are excluded from Spotlight, so file names inside them don't show up anywhere else.

We never see your card

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, a merchant of record. We receive only your email and order.

Why we charge.

“Free” apps often make their money by selling your data to brokers. We charge a small fee so we never have to.
