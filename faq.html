Does GlassDrive replace Finder?

It replaces how you browse, not Finder itself. macOS always needs Finder running for the desktop, so GlassDrive works alongside it on the same files. You can even make GlassDrive open whenever you open a folder, and switch back to Finder with one click.

Can I go back to how things were?

Yes. GlassDrive never moves your files into its own format. Every change can be reverted from Activity & Revert, deleted items go to the Trash, and if you stop using GlassDrive, Finder shows everything exactly where it was.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

Without it, macOS asks separately for your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, iCloud Drive and every external drive. One switch in System Settings covers them all, so you're not interrupted again. You can turn it off any time.

How do Confidential folders work?

Each one is an encrypted disk image protected by your password: AES-256, the same technology as Apple's Disk Utility. GlassDrive never stores the password. Without it the files can't be opened, not even by us. You can also open them from Finder by double-clicking and typing the password.

Will the Storage Offloader delete my files?

Only when you tell it to, or if you switch on automatic mode. Offloaded files go to the Trash, so you can put them back until you empty it. Starred and protected files are never touched.

Why can't I just use Apple's Finder and iCloud?

You can! They're free. Our cheeky theory is that the harder it is to see what's filling your Mac, the sooner you'll buy more iCloud storage or a new drive. GlassDrive puts your comfort first and helps you clear space instead. Read more.
